Exploring a focused sports-technology concept around pickleball

Fravara is an early-stage idea for an AI-enabled sports ecosystem focused on pickleball. The direction evolved from an earlier smart-glasses concept. The current priority is to understand the sport, identify a real user problem and decide whether an AI-enabled solution would add value.

Product thinkingUser needsConcept developmentAI exploration
Concept
No completed product prototype or validated user outcomes are claimed.

My contribution

  • Led the change in direction from the earlier wearable idea to a pickleball-focused concept.
  • Developed the current Fravara visual identity asset.
  • Started narrowing the concept around a specific sport and its potential user needs.
  • Identified user understanding and problem validation as priorities before committing to features or implementation.

Project focus

Explore whether a useful AI-enabled experience could support people involved in pickleball.

Current stage: problem exploration and product scoping, not a finished application.

Concept visual

Original concept artwork included in the supplied portfolio files.

A challenge and what I learned

The main challenge is avoiding a technology-first approach. Before deciding which AI features to build, I need to understand who the users are, what problem they have and whether AI is an appropriate solution.

This work is developing my product thinking, prioritisation and ability to communicate a concept's current maturity honestly.

Next steps

  1. Define the intended user groups and a clear problem statement.
  2. Gather feedback from pickleball participants.
  3. Prioritise one use case and sketch the user journey.
  4. Only then assess the technical approach and build a small prototype.

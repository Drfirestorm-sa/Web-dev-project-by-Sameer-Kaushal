Reflection & Evaluation

What is working well

The portfolio’s shared HTML, CSS and JavaScript structure helps keep navigation and presentation consistent across pages. The project pages also make it easier to explain the problem, approach and current status of each piece of work.

What I am improving

I am working to strengthen testing, document implementation decisions more clearly and distinguish completed features from experiments or ideas that are still under development.

Next steps

My next priorities are to gather stronger evidence for project outcomes, improve technical documentation and continue developing my programming, data science and AI skills.

Users, ethics & responsibility

I aim to start with the needs and context of the people who may use a system, rather than assuming that a technical feature is automatically useful. My AI projects have made me consider reliability, privacy, transparency and the risk of over-trusting generated outputs. Responsible development also means considering data protection, consent, accessibility, potential bias, copyright and software licences, and whether a proposed feature could create unintended social impacts. I should use data only for appropriate purposes, respect permissions and be clear about the limits of evidence before presenting a solution as ready for use.

Future direction

I am interested in AI, data science and software development. To develop in these areas, I plan to strengthen my programming and SQL skills, build more practical projects, improve my ability to evaluate technical solutions, and learn how to communicate technical ideas clearly.
